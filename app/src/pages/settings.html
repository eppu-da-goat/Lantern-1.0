    Privacy and security

    Safety check

    Checks your protection settings, threat lists and extensions.

      Dangerous site protection

      Warns you before scam and malware sites load. Lantern checks each address against open threat lists kept on your device, so the sites you visit aren't sent anywhere.

      Warn before risky downloads

      Asks before saving files that can run programs, or anything sent over an insecure connection. Downloaded files are also marked so your system checks them before they first open.

      Block trackers and ads

      Stops known tracking scripts and ad networks before they load.

      Block third-party cookies

      Sites can't read or set cookies on pages from other sites. A few sign-ins that rely on them may need a second try.

      Ask sites not to sell or share your data

      Sends Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track with every request.

      Always use secure connections

      Upgrades every site to HTTPS and falls back only when a site can't support it.

      Save browsing history

      Keeps a list of pages you visit on this device, for the History page and address bar suggestions. Private windows never save history.

        AI assistant

        API keys

        Add a key from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Groq to use the AI assistant. Paste a key, say which service it's for, and Lantern sends your questions straight to that service.

          Show the AI button

          The button at the top right of the toolbar and on Lantern Search. Its arrow switches between your AI services.

          How your keys are kept

          1. Keys are encrypted with your system keychain and stored only in this profile, on this device.
          2. A key is only ever sent to the service it belongs to. Lantern has no server in between.
          3. When you ask about a page, its text goes to the service you picked along with your question. Turn off "This page" in the panel to stop that.

          Search engine

          Search engine used in the address bar

          Lantern Search gives a cited summary on top of its results.

          Show summaries on Lantern Search

          Reads the top pages and quotes the key points, with sources. You can still summarize one search at a time when this is off.

          Search suggestions

          Suggests searches as you type in the address bar and search boxes. What you type is sent to DuckDuckGo without cookies; nothing is sent for web addresses.

          Complete addresses as you type

          Fills in sites from your history and bookmarks in the address bar, like typing "wik" for wikipedia.org.

          Mail link

          The Mail link at the top right of Lantern Search and the New Tab page.

          Remember recent searches

          Kept only on this device, in this profile. Turning this off forgets them. Clearing history also clears them.

          How Lantern Search works

          1. Your search goes to DuckDuckGo's index, without cookies and with tracking protection on.
          2. Lantern re-ranks the results on your device for variety and drops duplicates.
          3. For summaries, Lantern reads the top pages and picks sentences that answer your question, citing each one.

            About Lantern

            Lantern

            Lantern is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. It sends nothing about your browsing to anyone.

            Hardened build

            • Can't be relaunched as a plain Node.js process or debugged by other apps
            • Ignores code injected through environment variables
            • Loads only its own signed-off app archive, checked for tampering on Mac
            • Encrypts saved cookies and sign-ins with your system keychain
            • Warns before scam and malware sites, using threat lists checked on your device
            • Marks downloads so macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen check them before they run
            • Every website runs in its own sandboxed process (site isolation)

            No settings match your search.
